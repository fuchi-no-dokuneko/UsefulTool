Lightweight video compression

Change resolution, frame rate and encoding. Keep the full duration and audio, using the codecs available in your browser.

Aspect ratio is preserved and small videos are not enlarged. Audio is copied when compatible or re-encoded for the selected container. Smaller dimensions and FPS usually reduce size; already compressed videos can grow.
